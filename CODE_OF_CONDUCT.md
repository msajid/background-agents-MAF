## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and inclusive community for everyone, regardless
of background, identity, experience level, or ability.

## Expected behavior

* Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
* Assume good intent while addressing impact directly.
* Welcome questions and help newcomers learn.
* Give actionable feedback about ideas and code, not people.
* Respect privacy and never publish another person's sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, personal
attacks, and publication of private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the contact
options on their GitHub profiles. Do not open a public issue that identifies affected
people or contains private details.

Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or ban contributors when needed
to protect the community. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone represents the project in
public, including events, social media, and private project communications.
